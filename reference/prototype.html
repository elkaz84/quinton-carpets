What we sell

Come and have a look at our extensive ranges — hundreds of carpet samples to look at and feel, a large variety of vinyl, and high quality laminate.

From first look to fitted

Four steps, in this order.

Every step before the fitting is free. You only pay once you've chosen, in the shop.

STEP 01

Price it online

Pick a range, put your room sizes in and add underlay and fitting. You get an itemised estimate and a reference code in about two minutes.

STEP 02

Book a free measure

Choose a day and a time window. Booking costs nothing and commits you to nothing — we ring you back within one working day.

STEP 03

Choose in the shop

We measure up, then you see and feel the real thing on Hagley Road West and confirm the final price. Payment is taken in the shop, never online.

STEP 04

Our team fits it

Old flooring lifted and taken away, gripper and door bars fitted, everything hoovered before we leave. Then your referral code goes live.

New this year

Know the number before you come in.

The estimator works the way our fitters do: it works your rooms against 4m and 5m roll widths, adds the trimming allowance, then prices underlay, gripper, door bars and taking the old floor away — separately, so you can see what each part costs.

Refer a friend

One code. Two discounts.

Most of our work has always come from people telling their neighbours. This just makes it worth something.

STEP 01

Finish your fitting

Your personal code is issued the day the job is signed off. It's yours for good — there's no expiry.

STEP 02

Pass it on

Your friend puts the code in when they book a measure, or just reads it out in the shop. They take 10% off their fitting.

STEP 03

Claim your 20%

Once their job is fitted and paid, 20% off fitting on your next order lands against your code. Stack up as many as you like.

The small print, in plain English

What the discount applies to

Fitting and fitting extras — gripper, door bars, uplift and disposal. Materials are already at our best price, so they stay as they are.

When it's paid

Your friend's 10% comes off at the till on the day. Your 20% is held against your code and taken off your next invoice in the shop.

How many you can hold

No limit on how many friends you refer. One 20% discount can be used per order, and it can't be combined with a seasonal promotion.

Who can use a code

Anyone at a different address to yours, on their first order with us. We'll always tell you when one of your codes has been redeemed.
